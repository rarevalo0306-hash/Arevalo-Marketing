# MATYA

MATYA is Ricardo's marketing application, initially used for his own businesses, with a future goal of offering marketing services. The current authentication is for a shared owner account, not external client accounts.

The owner wants one autonomous marketing director with specialist research/SEO, writing and design/video capabilities. It must know the business and its industry, preserve source dates and geography, execute within configured limits, and support optional manual approval.

The public MATYA website and its existing 3D agent are separate from this application and are preserved. Existing campaign, brand, video, SEO, publishing, reporting and connection tools remain available. News can inform original content; third-party media needs documented reuse rights.

The new center is an extension of the existing visual language. Spanish is primary, English remains supported. Current work must be reviewable in a separate PR; production activation and a funded pilot are separate from local implementation and testing.
